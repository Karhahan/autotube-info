Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

AutoTube is an automated video creation and publishing tool for educational short videos.

Data Accessed

AutoTube accesses only your own YouTube channel upload permissions via the YouTube Data API v3 (youtube.upload) to publish videos to your authorized channel.

How Data Is Used and Stored

  • OAuth credentials and tokens are used solely to upload your own generated videos to your YouTube channel.
  • Authorization tokens are stored locally on your machine in token.json.
  • No personal data, channel data, or authorization tokens are sold, rented, or shared with third parties.
